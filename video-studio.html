Make a short video from a prompt

Describe a topic, add a title, choose a length, and create a downloadable vertical motion-graphics video. Your prompt is processed locally in this page.

What this version does: it creates animated text and graphic scenes inspired by your prompt. It does not generate realistic footage, images, voices, or music, and it does not send your prompt to an AI service.

How to use it

  • Write one clear idea or a short scene description.
  • Select a duration and press Create video.
  • When rendering finishes, choose Download video.
  • Video recording depends on your browser supporting canvas recording and WebM.

For genuine text-to-video generation (AI-created footage), this page would need a video-generation provider and a secure server-side integration. Never put a provider secret/API key in a public website’s JavaScript.
